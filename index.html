A Story Worth Knowing

BTS (Bangtan Sonyeondan), also known as the Bangtan Boys, is a South Korean boy band formed in 2010. The band consists of seven members: Jin, Suga, J-Hope, RM, Jimin, V, and Jungkook. who co-write and co-produce their work. Since their debut in 2013, BTS has become a global phenomenon, known for their meaningful lyrics, innovative music videos, and powerful performances. They have broken numerous records and have a dedicated fanbase known as the "ARMY". In addition to their musical achievements, BTS has also been recognized for their positive influence on society, promoting messages of self-love, mental health awareness, and social issues. They have been invited to speak at the United Nations and have received numerous awards for their contributions to music and culture. Overall, BTS is a group that has made a significant impact on the music industry and inspired millions of fans around the world with their music and message.
